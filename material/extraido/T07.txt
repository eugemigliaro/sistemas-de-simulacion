          - Simulations “experimentally validated” (by definition).


          - Direct way from [Real Data] —-> [Simulations] (No need to “guess” a model).


          - Increasingly relevant in light of the current AI revolution.


          - Computationally efficient (bigger “dt” that force-based models).
